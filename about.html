Company

One record for every contractor. That is the whole company.

Etyme builds the system a program office uses to see every contractor on its sites, which supplier sent them, and what they cost — and the rules that keep that record honest.

What we build

Companies that use staffing suppliers know their employees by name and their contractors by invoice. Etyme keeps one record for both sides of that gap: the role that was raised, the supplier that filled it, the contract it wrote, the weeks that were signed, the bills that matched, and the papers that were checked before day one.

Eight areas, in the order the work happens, read by six roles. Nobody sees a rate that is not theirs; everybody sees what is waiting on them.

How we work

Rules before people

A requirement clears by rule first and goes to a person only when a rule fails. Where the law is behind a rule, the product blocks. Everywhere else it warns, and records even a pass.

Nobody signs their own

A contractor never approves their own week. A manager never approves their own expenses. A bill with no signed week behind it is not paid.

Nothing we would take back

No borrowed customer logos. No analyst quadrant. No price we have not settled. What is on this site is checkable today.

Free to start, governance included

A program opens with no card and no clock. Compliance and tenure logic are part of every program, never a paid tier.
