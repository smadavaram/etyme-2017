Timesheets & expenses

The person files their own week, or the agency that employs them does. The manager who owns the work signs it, nobody approves their own hours, and a week that looks wrong is flagged before anybody signs.

Key capabilities

File once, against the contract

By the person or their employer. Never twice for one week.

Nobody signs their own

Only the paying company signs, and rejection says why.

Signed twice

Client signs so supplier invoices. Employer signs so person gets paid.

Flagged before anyone signs

Over 12 hours a day, over 60 a week, over the role, or past contract end. Warns, never blocks.

Reminders before due

At 7, 3, and 1 day out.

Auto-sign quiet weeks

Only if your company turns it on, and never if flagged.

The complaint

“Every Monday I get four spreadsheets in four formats. I sign what I am sent, because the alternative is finding the person and asking. Then the invoice arrives with different hours.”

A hiring manager who signs for contractors from three suppliers

The hours were right when the person wrote them down. They were re-keyed by the supplier, summarised by the prime and copied into the invoice, and each copy was a chance to be wrong. The manager signs the second copy and pays the fourth.

What Etyme does

A week is filed once, against the contract, by the person or by the agency that employs them, and cannot be filed twice for the same period. Submitting locks it. Only the company being billed can approve it, nobody approves their own hours, and rejection needs a reason. The signed week carries two signatures: the client approves it, so the supplier may invoice, and the employer accepts it, so the person can be paid. Accepting different hours needs a reason too.

Before anyone signs, the sheet is read. More than twelve hours in a day or sixty in a week, more than the role allows, or a day past the contract’s end is flagged and shown first. Flags warn; they never block. A reminder goes to the person seven, three and one day before the week is due. Approving a silent week automatically exists, is off until your company turns it on, and never touches a flagged sheet. Expenses are filed by the supplier against the contract, in six categories, and only approved, billable expenses reach an invoice.
