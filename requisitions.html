Requisitions & suppliers

A manager says what they need. Most requirements clear the moment they are raised; only those over plan, over budget or above the going rate go to a person. Once approved, a role goes only to the suppliers Procurement cleared, each at its own rate band.

Key capabilities

Raise in plain terms

A role, a budget, a rate band and a cost center. Most open the moment they are raised.

Rules clear first

Headcount plan, budget and the going rate are checked by rule. A desk sees it only when one fails.

Three desks, in order

HR for the role, Procurement for sourcing, the cost-center lead last. Nobody signs their own.

Release to cleared suppliers only

Procurement names the firms. Each gets its own rate band and cannot see anyone else’s.

Invitations that expire

Fourteen days by default, so a role never sits with a firm that went quiet.

Supplier onboarding, four desks

Lead, Procurement, HR, Finance. An eleven-item checklist, then probation, approved or preferred.

The complaint

“A hiring manager emails three agencies she likes. Procurement finds out when the invoices arrive, and one of the three was never approved to work with us at all.”

The procurement desk at a company with more suppliers than it thinks it has

Nobody in that story did anything wrong by their own lights. The manager needed somebody. The agencies answered. The rule that says which firms may be used lived in a document, and a document cannot stop an email.

What Etyme does

A requirement is raised with a budget, a rate band and a cost center. Rules read it first: the headcount plan, the budget, the rate against the going rate for that skill. If every check passes it opens straight away. If one fails, it goes to the desk that owns that check: HR for the role, Procurement for the sourcing, and the cost-center lead last. A lead who raised it cannot approve it; it goes one level up.

When Procurement approves, it names the suppliers it cleared, and only those firms can be sent the role. Each supplier gets its own rate band and cannot see anyone else’s. A firm becomes a supplier the same way: your lead confirms the need, Procurement qualifies it, HR clears compliance, Finance approves it, and whoever recommended the firm sits on none of the four desks.
