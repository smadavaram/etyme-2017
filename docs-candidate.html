Position on a deal

You granted a bench listing to one firm — the consent that lets it put you forward — and can take it back. That firm pays you, by payroll or corp-to-corp. A prime above it may sell you on to a client that thinks the prime employs you. You file your own week and nobody else may; you are told your pay and never what anybody above you charges.
