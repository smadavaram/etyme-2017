Contracts & onboarding

The award writes the contract, with the person, the rate and the dates on it, against the order that holds the ceiling. Before day one the papers are checked: work authorization and insurance block a start; the rest warns and takes a reason.

Key capabilities

The award writes the contract

Person, bill rate, dates, hiring manager and requisition, on a sell contract and a buy contract.

An order with a ceiling

Raised wherever a ceiling can be stated. Nothing is invoiced past it.

Four ways to be employed

W-2 through the supplier, their own company, a sole trader, or contract to hire. A type you do not accept is a block.

Papers that block a start

Right to work, liability and workers’ compensation cover, and a professional license where the role needs one.

Papers that warn

Background check, drug screen, NDA. The contract can start with a reason recorded.

Start confirmed on site

Somebody says the person arrived, and billing stands on a fact.

The complaint

“The contract said one rate, the purchase order said another, and the person started three weeks before anyone found the I-9 was missing.”

The onboarding desk at a company with contractors from four suppliers

Three documents written by three people from three copies of the same conversation. Each was right when it was written. Nothing tied them to each other, so nothing noticed when they drifted, and the person on site was the last to know.

What Etyme does

Awarding a candidate writes the paperwork. The sell contract carries the person, the bill rate, the dates, the hiring manager and the requisition. The buy contract carries how the person is employed: W-2 through the supplier, their own company, a sole trader, or contract to hire. An order is raised with the ceiling wherever a ceiling can be stated. The rate on the contract is the rate that was awarded, and there is no second copy to drift.

A contract is activated only when the papers clear. Right to work, general liability and workers’ compensation cover, and a professional license where the role needs one, block a start until they are on file. A background check or an NDA warns, and the contract can start with a reason recorded. Then somebody confirms the person is on site, and billing stands on a fact.
