Logo

The wordmark is “etyme” in ink. The crossbar of the t is replaced by three rising strokes in violet, orange and green; that stroke on its own is the mark. On a dark band the letters turn white and the strokes stay. Height is 26px in the site header, 22px in a running head, 28px for the mark beside a rail. Leave clear space of one x-height around it. Do not recolour, stretch, add a gradient, or put it in a circle.

4

Buttons

One primary button per screen. Secondary buttons are white with a rule border. Tertiary buttons are text. The danger button is only for something that cannot be undone. Corners are 8px, text is 14px semibold, small is 13px, large is 15px.

Hover darkens the violet to #3F16E8, pressing to #3512C4 and moves the button down 1px. Loading hides the label and shows a spinner. Disabled is 45% and keeps its colour. Focus is a 2px violet outline, 2px out, on everything that can take the keyboard.

13

Layout and motion

Layout

  • Page width 1180px on the site, 1200px inside the dashboard, with 20–24px side gutters. On a phone the gutter is 16px and nothing scrolls sideways except a table or a drawing in its own box.
  • Rail 186–220px, surface, a rule on its right. It hides under 900px and comes back as a drawer from the menu button.
  • Breakpoints 900px (rail folds, header collapses to a drawer), 820px (grids go to one column), 480px (tiles go to one column).
  • Spacing 8px steps. Groups use gap, not margins. Cards pad 22px, tiles 14px, table cells 12px by 10px.
  • Corners 8px on most things, 12px on shells and overlays, 6px on navigation items, 5px on chips, 4px on boxes and keys, pill on filters and badges.

Motion and states

  • Durations 120ms for colour, 180ms for shape, 260ms for anything entering the screen. One curve: cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1).
  • Hover fills with sunk or surface; a clickable card lifts 2px with the lift shadow. Press moves a button down 1px.
  • Focus is a 2px violet outline offset 2px, on every control, never removed.
  • Entering a menu pops in 120ms; a modal or toast slides up 260ms; a drawer slides in from the right.
  • Reduced motion turns every transition off.

14

Words

Plain English, sentence case, no metaphors. A screen says what it is for in its first line. A rule is one sentence in the present tense that names who and what: “Nobody signs their own week.” A button says the verb and the thing: “Raise requisition”, “Export CSV”, never “Submit” or “OK”. Numbers keep their unit and their date. An empty screen says what belongs there and how to add the first one. Errors say what to do next, not what went wrong inside.

Say

  • Waiting on the client’s approver since Sep 22.
  • The PO has $2,400 left. This invoice is $3,100.
  • No suppliers yet. Invite the first one.
  • Signed by Northbend Athletic, Sep 22, 4:10 pm.

Do not say

  • Pending approval workflow.
  • Insufficient PO balance (error 4102).
  • Let’s get started on your supplier journey!
  • Status: APPROVED ✓
