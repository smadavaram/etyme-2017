Nothing to set up

These are in place when you sign up. Each one is something you can check on the page afterward.

  • An address on Etyme. Yours looks like yourfirm.etyme.com. A web address of your own is optional, and comes later.
  • Drawn from your record. There is no second site to keep up. Change the record and the page follows.
  • Only people who said yes. A person is shown by skills and availability, and only after her own yes.
  • Replies land on your record. A request becomes a candidate or a submission you can act on, not a message you must find.
  • Your colors and logo. You upload them, or Etyme reads them from your logo. The page says "Runs on Etyme" in small type.
  • Take it down any time. Publishing is one yes, and so is taking the page down.

It looks like your firm, not like Etyme

The page uses your logo, your two colors and your fonts. If a color is hard to read, Etyme says so and leaves your choice as it is. You can switch the layout later without losing anything.

After you publish

The page is live and has its own link. Then Etyme drafts a message for each way you reach people: email to clients you know, WhatsApp, a link for job sites, and your chat channels. You read each draft and say yes or not now.

Questions

Can a visitor see my people's names or rates?

No. A visitor sees role, skills, place and the date she is free. Names and rates are never on the page.

Does Etyme send anything without my yes?

No. Every draft is a card with Yes and Not now. Nothing leaves until you press Yes.

Do I need my own website first?

No. The page lives on Etyme and is drawn from your record. Add your own address later if you want one.

Where do the replies go?

Onto your record, as candidates and submissions, with who asked and what they asked for.

What if my firm does more than one thing?

Show all three shelves, or any one. Say "show consulting only" and the page changes.
