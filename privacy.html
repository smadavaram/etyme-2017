Privacy policy

What Etyme collects, why, how long it is kept, and what you can ask us to do with it. Written to be read, not skimmed past.

Draft for legal review   Effective date to be set on approval.

1. Who we are

Etyme Inc., 3201 Yorktown Ave, Suite 110B, Durham, NC 27713, United States (“Etyme”, “we”). For the information you give us on this website and for your account, we decide how it is used. For the information a customer enters into its program — its contractors, suppliers, rates, weeks and papers — the customer decides, and we process it on the customer’s instructions under the data processing addendum.

2. What we collect

  • What you type on this site. An email address, and if you choose to give them, your name, your company, and what you wrote in the box. We also keep which page the form was on and when it was sent.
  • Your account. The email a sign-in link is sent to, when each link was sent and used, and a log of every screen on which a person’s data was read. There is no password to store.
  • Program data. Everything a customer and its suppliers enter to run a program: requisitions, submissions, contracts, timesheets, expenses, invoices, and compliance documents. This belongs to the customer.
  • Technical records. The address and browser a request came from, and the time it arrived, kept for security and to keep the service running.

This website does not carry advertising trackers, and we do not buy information about you from anyone.

3. Why we use it

  • To run your program and show each person only what their seat is allowed to see.
  • To answer what you asked. If you leave a question, one person reads it and writes back. Nobody is added to a list, and nothing is sent that you did not ask for.
  • To send a sign-in link when you ask for one.
  • To keep the service secure, to find and fix faults, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use program data to build anything other than your program.

4. Who else sees it

The companies that host the service and deliver our email see data only as far as that job requires, under contracts that bind them to this policy. The current list is kept in the documentation under Sub-processors and changes are noted there before they take effect. Within a program, your suppliers see the requisitions released to them and the weeks and invoices they filed; they never see another supplier’s rate. We disclose information to authorities only where the law requires it, and we say so where the law allows.

5. How long we keep it

Program data is kept for as long as the program is active and for a defined period afterwards so the customer can export it. Each category of record has its own period, listed in the documentation under Retention, by category. Sign-in and read logs are kept for the period the customer’s policy sets. What you typed on this site is kept until your question is answered and for a short period after, then deleted.

6. How it is protected

Data travels encrypted between your browser and the service and is stored encrypted at rest. Access inside Etyme is limited to the people who need it to support you, and every read of a person’s data is logged. Our security position in the documentation says what is done, what is not, and when.

7. What you can ask

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, object to a use of it, or ask for it in a file you can take elsewhere. Every screen in a program exports to CSV. Write to support@etyme.com and a person will answer within a reasonable time. If your information sits inside a customer’s program, we will pass your request to that customer, who decides.

8. Where it is processed

Etyme is based in the United States and processes data there. Where a customer is elsewhere and the law requires particular terms for the transfer, those terms are in the data processing addendum.

9. Children

The service is for businesses and the people who work with them. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and customers with a program are told before a material change takes effect. Questions go to support@etyme.com or to Etyme Inc., 3201 Yorktown Ave, Suite 110B, Durham, NC 27713.
