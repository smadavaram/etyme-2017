Position on a deal

A position on a deal, not a kind of firm: the rung below a prime. Sells to the prime at a rate of its own; the prime sells on at another. Knows the site it works at — it must, for tenure and compliance — and the platform carries no thread from it to a client it has no deal with, which is the other half of the NDA between it and the prime.

The same drawing as the prime, shifted one rung down: the sub’s customer is the prime, the end client is behind the prime’s name, and the sub’s own desks do the same jobs. What the sub never sees is the rate above it.
