## A firm on Etyme is never tied to you from your side alone — the founder, 2026-11-13

Found by three reviewers and then three skeptics reading the neutral
Company change (record 60), and held as a rule from this day.

**The rule.** Two firms on Etyme link by one ask and one yes. No door
writes an agreement, a register row, an order, a contract line, a job, a
submission or a bank mark on a firm that did not take part. No reply
tells a caller about a firm it does not work with: no id, no stored
name, no Etyme address. A firm you do not deal with is answered exactly
like an id that does not exist, and the refusal is logged before it is
sent.

**What counts as dealing with a firm.** An agreement between the two; a
register row the OTHER firm wrote naming you; an order; a contract line;
a job the OTHER firm sent you; or a yes to an ask to link. A row or a
job you wrote yourself never counts.

**What counts as a firm taking part in a client's supplier approval.**
One of the firm's own signed-in people, holding the right to keep its
list of companies, answered the client's application link; or the two
already deal with each other; or the firm is the client's own unclaimed
record.

**Decided by the founder, 2026-11-13.** Asked whether a firm that
answers the application link from its own verified email domain, without
signing in, counts as taking part, so that the client's approval attaches
it directly. His answer, on the recommendation to leave it as it is:
*"Keep it that way."* So it does not count. Anyone holding the link can
answer it. The approval still reads Approved on the client's side,
nothing is written on the firm, and the approver presses "Ask them to
link"; the firm becomes a supplier on its own yes.

**The doors this closed**, each with test sentences: Add a company and
the register route; Extend to Supplier and to Customer; the supplier
paste box; the supplier approval and its bank check; the duplicate-join
door; sending a job by id and on the last approval; sending a person on
by id; contract lines, orders and agreements; the purchase-order sheet
import and the contractor import; the ask to link and its yes.

**Still open, for the next deploy.** A firm approved before it took part
has nothing the four roles verified on its compliance record after its
yes (the writer exists, `evidenceOnLinkYes`, and nothing calls it yet).
`workTogether` still counts a job invitation written from one side
before this rule. A repaste of a firm the client already lists, that has
since signed up, reads it as a stranger. One-sided ties written on
production by the old paths are not cleaned up.
