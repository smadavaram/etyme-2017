# 132. Plugin 5 is public-sector proposals and supplier diversity — 2026-11-27

## The ruling, relayed by Pax (architect) from the owner's Nov 26 widening

Plugin 5 covers public-sector proposals **and** supplier diversity:
- MBE, WBE, 8(a) and HUBZone certifications;
- corporate supplier-diversity registrations and portals;
- diversity set-aside bids.

Tapin will be registered on Etyme as a minority-owned business and bids
through its own tenant door. Everything is done in the name of the legal
entity the Owner records (Etyme Inc. for Tapin). The certification is not
yet held, and a certification is claimed only once it is held. The agent
drafts only; a human approves and submits.

## Pax's answers the same day

- "The firm's own domain" in the website switch checks is the storefront's
  own verified web address, not the sign-in domain.
- The proposals agent never finds bids itself. A person records each
  opportunity.

## How it is built

- A certification is a record on the tenant: kind, certifier, status
  (not held, applied, held, lapsed), number, dates and the certificate as
  a paper. Only "held" with a certificate on file and in date may be
  claimed anywhere.
- A registration on a buyer's supplier-diversity portal is a record the
  agent drafts answers for; a person submits on the portal.
- A set-aside bid names the certification it requires. Where the tenant
  does not hold it, the proposal says so and is not put up for approval
  as eligible.
- No legal entity name is written in code; it is the Owner's entry on the
  tenant's record. Ownership status (minority-owned) is the Owner's
  attestation, never the agent's.
