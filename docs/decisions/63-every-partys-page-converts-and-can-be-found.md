## Every party's page converts, looks like the home page, and can be found — the founder, 2026-11-13

**The founder's words.** *"Think like marketing and branding head -
build should basically help converting. Conversion to customers from
clients to supply to candidates"*. And: *"Make every page inside convert
and brand similar and easily findable by others - for example if I am
bench vendor. Home page will still target client"*.

**What stands.**

1. **The home page still speaks to the client.** Nothing on it is aimed
   at a supplier. One quiet line may send another reader to their own
   page: "Not a client? Suppliers · Bench vendors · MSPs · Integrators ·
   Workers".
2. **Every other party's page takes the home page's shape and brand**:
   its own headline, its own named person doing its own step, one call
   to act, the same panels, type and colors.
3. **Each page sells that party its own next step.** A client brings its
   contractor list. A supplier answers its client's invitation. A bench
   vendor lists who is available. A worker says yes to being shown. The
   chain is client, then supply, then candidates: each party that joins
   brings the next.
4. **Each page can be found by the party it is for.** A bench vendor
   reaches its page from the footer in one step, by a plain address, and
   from a search engine: a title and description of its own, a sitemap,
   a canonical address and a card for sharing. The app's own screens
   stay out of search.
5. **Sign-up knows which party came in**, from the page it was opened
   on, and never asks the visitor to classify themselves (record 42).
6. The rules of record 03 hold on every page: no size range, no
   invented customer, no price on a door, neutrality, and nothing that
   reads as Etyme running a bench.

**Found by the inventory, 2026-11-13.** No sitemap, no robots file, no
canonical addresses and no plain addresses exist. A bench vendor reaches
its words only by the footer, then Suppliers, then the Bench tab. The
home page's parts are written inside the page and must be lifted out
before another page can use them. Fifteen tests will move.

**Not built.** It is the second deploy of this wave, after the first is
out. Order: the bench vendor's page first, drawn and photographed at
phone width; then the other doors; then the product pages. Beside it,
four conversion fixes inside the app: the client's first act reads
"Bring your contractor list"; an invited supplier and an invited
candidate land on a page that says who asked and what happens next; an
approver at a client that is not on Etyme reads one line after
approving; and the funnel is counted.
