## The profit logic the pack must serve — 2026-11-21

**What it is.** A note from the tester, at the owner's direction, that
sits on top of the execution pack (record 105). The pack is the build
order; this is why. Where the two pull apart, the pack's guardrails
win: no agent sends, no agent decides money, and no agent acts across
companies. The named note stays outside the repository, as the pack
does.

**The arithmetic, with its assumptions named.** A placed consultant at
a $15 an hour margin over about 1,880 billable hours is about $28,000
to $30,000 of gross profit a year. One million dollars is then 33 to 36
consultant-years. At a $10 margin it is about 50. Holding the margin is
worth more than adding volume. These are the note's own model figures,
not a promise and not for any public page.

**Where the gap is.** Not supply and not demand: more requirements
arrive each day than the firm reads. The gap is conversion and leakage.
1. A rate confirmation is issued and no outcome is ever recorded.
2. A rate is won in writing and then drifts, by a vendor's notice.
3. Money is billed and does not arrive: old invoices, short pays,
   recoveries nobody decides.
4. The people who close spend their day on one-line clerical acts.

**The levers, in order, each already a pack item.**
1. Close the confirmation loop: every confirmation gets an outcome, so
   the firm knows its submission-to-placement rate.
2. Answer fast, on the record.
3. Hold the rate: agreed against actual, drift as a same-week decision.
4. Collect as one chain: no invoice past 60 days without a named owner
   and a next step.
5. Put the verified bench to work: each day's requirements matched
   against it, as a suggestion.
6. Agents do the clerical work; people close.

**The direction beyond this tranche** (not to be built now).
- The record is what lasts; a model can be rented by anybody. The pack
  is built so that history accumulates as a by-product of the work:
  every rate, outcome, drift and payment.
- Rate guidance at the moment of confirmation, from the firm's own
  outcomes. A person still confirms.
- Scorecards of who pays on time and who drifts, from the ledger.
- A chain that produces a measured result here becomes a standard
  offer.
- Never: an agent that sends or negotiates by itself; people's data
  scraped into a bench; a one-off workflow per client.

**To be measured from the ledger and reported with each batch:**
conversion (submissions to placements), rate drift caught, and the age
of money owed.

**Where it meets the standing rules.**
- "No feature ever compares a supplier's rates across clients." Rate
  guidance and scorecards are read only inside the one tenant, from its
  own records, and nothing crosses to another tenant. They are not in
  this tranche; before either is built, the founder says so.
- A result measured at one tenant is that tenant's. It is named on a
  public page only with its consent, and only as a figure a reader can
  check.

### The owner's planning assumption: no loan — 2026-11-21

"Assume [the second firm] carries no loan, and perform on that basis."
It is a planning assumption the owner gave, not a fact this record
checked.
- Collection is fuel, not defence. Money collected is working capital
  to use, not a hole to fill. Any earlier framing of slow collection as
  a threat to the firm's survival is struck. This record never carried
  that framing.
- The freed cash has a job: carrying strong W-2 consultants between
  placements, so they are not placed at a poor rate to end a gap. That
  protects the margin the model assumes, and keeps a verified bench
  available when a requirement is matched.
- The firm may walk away from a drifting rate. Holding a rate is a
  choice it can afford.
- The model's arithmetic stands. With no interest between gross margin
  and profit, the review at day 90 reports operating profit directly.
- *One field for the money loop* (record 105): a chain of money owed to
  the firm records, once collected, where the money was put: bench
  carry, operations, or reserve. A person decides that and records it
  on the chain. The agent still only drafts. Etyme still never moves
  money: the field is a record of a decision, not an instruction to a
  bank.
- Every guardrail of the pack stands unchanged.

### Two corrections from the owner — 2026-11-21

**1. The loan is the sister firm's.** The second firm is not the
borrower. "No loan" is therefore a fact the owner states, not a
supposition. The risk runs the other way: the second firm is the
creditor, and its largest sum owed is owed by the firm that carries the
loan.
- In the money loop, that chain carries a counterparty-risk flag.
- The pace of collection from that firm, and any further credit given
  to it, are decision-queue items for a person, never routine chases.
- Everything else in this record stands: bench carry, the rate
  posture, and the field for where collected money was put.
- The lender, the amounts and the people stay outside the repository.

**2. The mission, in the owner's words.** Scale the second firm to 36
consultants at an average margin of $15 an hour within 365 days, from
2026-11-21 to 2027-11-21. 36 × $15 × about 1,880 billable hours is
about $1.0 million of gross profit.
- The two headline measures are the count of consultants billing and
  the blended margin an hour. They lead the agent control view and the
  trajectory report.
- The baseline is an output of the census (pack batch 2), never an
  assumption. Until the census is loaded, both read "not yet measured".
- Quarter one is 2026-11-22 to 2027-02-19: the engine built and
  measured, the ramp begun. The day-90 review reports against 36 at $15
  by 2027-11-21, with the quarterly ramp drawn from the measured
  baseline.
- Each agent is judged by whether it moves one of the two measures:
  more consultants billing, or margin held.

**Where the headline meets a standing rule.** Margin is closed to most
seats: the Account Manager reads no profit and loss, HR reads no money,
and a recruiter reads no margin unless the Owner grants it. The blended
margin is drawn only for a seat that may read margin; another seat
sees the count and not the margin.
