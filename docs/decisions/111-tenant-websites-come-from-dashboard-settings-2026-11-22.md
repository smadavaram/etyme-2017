## Tenant websites come from the dashboard settings — 2026-11-22

**The owner's ruling.** Tapin's and the second firm's websites are not
built directly as one-off builds. A tenant's website is made from its
dashboard settings. The settings-driven website engine is built in
Etyme once. The tenant configures its own site in Settings: the
template, the branding, the pages and the content, all stored on the
tenant's record. The public site renders from those settings. Tapin and
the second firm are configurations of that engine, never their own
builds. Work that builds a tenant's site directly stops, and the
settings path is built in its place.
