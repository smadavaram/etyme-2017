# 116 — Agent outreach follows a mass-mail service's policy (2026-11-24)

## The owner's words

Asked whether an agent may send outreach to a dormant customer or
supplier after a person's yes, he answered: "Think [a mass-mail
service] and follow their policy." The service is named in the
conversation, not here, by the rule that no real company is named in a
file.

## The lead's reading

An agent may send outreach to a dormant customer or supplier after one
person's yes on the task card (record 112, one yes per task), under that
service's acceptable-use policy as read on 2026-11-24:

1. **Permission.** Only to a contact the firm itself recorded, at a
   company the firm has traded with. Past trade counts as permission,
   as that policy says purchase history does. Never a bought, rented,
   third-party, scraped or public list, and never a contact read from
   another firm's record.
2. **Unsubscribe in every letter.** Every outreach letter carries a
   working unsubscribe that needs no sign-in. Once used, that contact
   gets no outreach from that firm again. It never stops a chase or a
   bill, which are about a deal already made.
3. **Honest sender.** The letter names the firm that sends it and its
   postal address, and its subject says what it is.
4. **Bounces suppress.** An address whose outreach bounced gets no
   outreach again until a person records a new address.
5. **Volume.** At most one outreach letter per company and side in 180
   days (record 115's cooling-off, still a proposal), under the agent's
   daily cap.

Chases and final notices are about a deal already made, not marketing.
The final notice stays drafted for a person to send until the owner says
otherwise, and the chase numbers stay proposals.
