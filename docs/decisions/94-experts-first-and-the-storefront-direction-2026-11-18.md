## Experts first, and the storefront direction — 2026-11-18

**What he said, on decisions.** "Etyme 2017 did not have a team of
functional analysts or domain experts. Claude should bring that for me
to make decisions." And before it, on a list of twelve questions: "these
questions are coming to me?"

**The rule.**
- A question reaches the founder only after the domain experts have
  answered it. The experts are the domain agents, run as read-only
  advisers: money for payroll and billing practice, regulatory for the
  law and its sources, architect for the build and its risk, and the
  others by subject.
- Each adviser answers recommendation first, with the one deciding
  reason, what could go wrong, and the source to confirm it with. An
  adviser writes no code.
- What the experts settle, and what the standing rules already decide,
  the lead decides and records. The founder can overrule any of it.
- What still reaches him is a decision brief: the choice in one
  sentence, what the experts agree on, where they differ, and one
  recommendation. Money arithmetic, legal exposure, public words and
  product direction still reach him, as briefs.
- A legal point is never enforced on an adviser's word alone. Regulatory
  confirms it with its source first, as before.

**The first use, the same day.** Three advisers answered the open pay
questions. Money and regulatory agreed: a week worked abroad with no
recorded rule should be paid at straight time on schedule, with only
the overtime premium held, because holding undisputed wages is itself
late payment in most places. That changes answer 4 of record 92, so it
waits for his yes. The current hold stays until then. A per-country
rule table comes before any release action. The architect ranked the
queued builds and set defaults for six small items.

**What he said, on storefronts.** The 2017 storefronts "never sold" and
are not a model. Storefronts are for staffing, contracting and
consulting. For marketing and storefronts the model is a
prompt-to-product builder he named: one box, say what you want, see it
live, publish with one click. For transacting the model is an
agent-first system of record he named: the agent does the work on the
record and a person says yes. "Ease of life is the core."

**How it is read against the standing rules.**
- One storefront with three shelves, chosen by the Owner's one sentence:
  staffing (people by skills, place and start date), contracting (one
  person's availability) and consulting (an offer, with "Request a
  scope" and no price). A firm is never asked to classify itself.
- The Owner describes the firm, Etyme drafts the storefront from the
  firm's own record, the Owner edits in plain words, and one yes
  publishes it. The storefront is in the tenant's branding (record 79).
- Etyme drafts each outgoing message and picks the channel. A person's
  yes sends it. No model output sends anything (record 81). Replies land
  on the record. Only people who said yes are shown, never a name or a
  rate.
- The client home page does not change. The box goes on the firm-facing
  door, until he says otherwise.
- No real company is named on any page, in the repository or in a
  reply.

**What is running.** A static prototype of four screens: the door, the
build, the published storefront and the share step. Nothing is built
until he has seen it.

### His answer on a week abroad — 2026-11-18

"A week abroad does not matter. Salary is always in the home country
unless the base is changed."
- A trip, of any length, moves nothing: pay and overtime follow the home
  employment. This is answer 4 of record 92, said again.
- Only a changed base matters. The base is the work location on the
  line, and only a person moves it. The hold reads that location and
  nothing else, so a week of travel is never held.
- Still open, for a line whose base was moved to a country with no
  recorded rule: hold the whole week, as built, or pay straight time and
  hold only the premium, as the advisers recommend.

### His limit on the storefront — 2026-11-18

"Don't touch the Etyme home page. The storefront is only one of the
tenant gateway products. The core is still their company contracts,
globally, at one spot."
- The home page stays as his brief of 2026-11-12 left it. No storefront
  work edits it, and the storefront box never goes on it.
- The storefront is one gateway a tenant may use, beside the others. It
  is never the lead of the product, of a public page for clients, or of
  the order of work.
- The core stays the system of record: every contract a company has
  with its contingent workers and suppliers, in every country, in one
  place.
