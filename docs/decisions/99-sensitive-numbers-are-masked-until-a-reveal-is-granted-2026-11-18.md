## Sensitive numbers are masked until a reveal is granted — 2026-11-18

**What he said** (message 132, as formalized for him and sent on). It
covers a tax ID, bank details and a social security number.
1. **Masked and encrypted, always.** Stored encrypted and shown masked
   on every screen, for everyone, the Owner included, until a reveal is
   granted.
2. **The Owner's reveal.** Click to display, but only after signing in
   again at that moment: the same second check as a money act, passkey
   first and the authenticator app as fallback.
3. **Anyone else.** She asks, the Owner approves, and the value shows
   only inside the window the Owner grants. Then it is masked again.
   Nothing is unmasked for good.
4. **Sharing outside** (a payroll provider, an e-signature app). The
   owner of record confirms digitally. The file or form that carries
   the data is attached to the record with who confirmed, what was
   shared, with whom and when.
5. **Every reveal and every share is on the ledger**, on the record.

It stacks on the model rule: this data reaches no model on any
provider (records 81, 96).

  after a fresh second check, each showing logged". The permitted seat
