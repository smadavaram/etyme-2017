# 119 — A client never reaches below its supplier (2026-11-25)

## The owner's words

Asked, after the papers-view walk, whether a hidden sub-vendor's opaque
id should also be withheld from a client, and whether a client may open
the line between its supplier and that supplier's sub-vendor: "yes to
both - thats non-compete".

## The rule

1. **No trace of a hidden firm reaches a client**: not its name, not its
   id, not a file name or a reference that carries it. A client reads
   "your supplier" for the firm it pays and nothing below it, unless its
   agreement with that firm requires disclosure (the rule of 2026-09-17).
   The route's own comment that "a client cannot turn an id into a firm"
   no longer stands: an id is withheld with the name.
2. **A client cannot open the line between its supplier and a firm
   below it.** That line is a deal between two other firms. The client
   is refused it in one sentence, the refusal is logged first, and the
   answer is shaped as not found. The client reads its own line, with
   the standing of whoever employs the person on its site, as before.

The reason is the non-compete between the prime and the sub-vendor:
nothing Etyme shows a client may let it reach round its supplier to the
firm below.
