## The lead's design (the owner left it to the lead)

Outreach is one engine with one tab under Candidates and one under
Companies, built in this order of steps:

1. **Audience.** A saved list read from the firm's own record: dormant
   customers, suppliers by skill, contacts by kind. Never a bought,
   rented, scraped or public list; never another firm's contacts; never
   a company "Not classified".
2. **Offer.** What the firm sells, drafted from its record: a hot list
   of who is available (skills and availability only, never a name or
   a rate, as the public bench rule says), a project pod, an open job,
   or a course (candidates only).
3. **Letter.** Plain text, drafted by Etyme, edited by a person. No
   designer, no tracking pixel.
4. **One yes per batch.** A person approves the batch once (record 112
   reads one task as one batch); each contact gets their own letter
   through the mail door, never more than ten addresses on one email.
5. **Record 116 for every letter**: unsubscribe link, the firm and its
   postal address, an honest subject, bounced addresses suppressed.
6. **Follow-up.** At most two follow-ups, stopped by a reply or an
   unsubscribe. A reply becomes a lead card on the decision queue.
7. **Landing.** Every link goes to the firm's own storefront (record
   79/94), which already carries the Ask box. No separate funnel
   builder.
8. **Learn.** Per batch: replies, unsubscribes, bounces and the leads
   they produced; the next offer and audience are suggested on a card
   a person approves.

**Candidates** get outreach only after their own yes to updates, with
their own unsubscribe; a bench yes is consent to be put forward, not to
be marketed to. Until that opt-in exists, the Candidates tab sends
nothing.
