## Company agents come first — 2026-11-18

**What he said.** After three ideas the same day (a blind-copy address
for recruiters, a mail and chat plugin he called a harnesser, and a
personal agent like one he admires): "I am more interested in company
agents for accounts, recruiters, HR, contracts, timesheets, billing,
invoice receipt and money flow."

**What it means.**
- The agents that matter first work for a company's own roles. This is
  the twin and its role contract, carried through to every role.
- The recruiter's mail plugin and the worker's own agent are parked.
  What the advisers said about them is kept below, so it is not paid for
  twice.

**What exists today** (read from the code, not run).
- Fourteen acts, none of which calls a model. They work with no key.
- Invoice receipts: the three-way verdict, the mismatch notice, and
  payment put up for a yes.
- Billing: the bill drafted on the agreement's date, and sending put up
  for a yes.
- Recruiter: a bench person put forward for a yes, and who is coming
  free.
- HR: start papers asked for, and the nightly document chases.
- A client's program manager: the time-limit warnings.
- Nothing of its own for the account manager, contracts, timesheets or
  money flow. Two acts, the reminder and the summary, are in the role
  contract and nothing calls them.
- One job runs once a night. No task keeps a state between runs, no seat
  has its own queue, and nothing runs on a clock under a day.

**The smallest first batch, rules only, on readers that exist.**
1. Timesheets: remind the late filer and the late approver.
2. Contracts: lines and agreements ending soon, to the account manager
   and HR.
3. Billing and money flow: overdue bills flagged, and a weekly cash
   summary from the forecast.
4. Invoice receipts: tell the AP clerk when a receipt waits on a bank
   check.
5. One daily summary each for the account manager, the recruiter and HR.

**Standing rules that hold.** Rules first, the model second. An agent
never asks the person whose authority it acts on, and never approves its
own draft. It never releases payroll, signs, awards, extends, rejects or
ends a placement. Every act is on the ledger. Every decision is a card
with Yes and Not now, on the one list each seat already reads.

**Parked, with what the advisers said.**
- *Blind copy to Etyme.* Per thread only, jobs only, through the closed
  forwarding door, switched on by the Owner. No "80%" figure: a "worth
  working" band with reasons, and the firm's own record once about
  thirty outcomes exist.
- *Mail and chat plugin.* Only the shape that reads the one message she
  opens keeps "Etyme never reads a mailbox". Marking the inbox needs a
  header read of the whole mailbox and a change to that rule. Background
  reading is refused. Her profile is learned from her acts in Etyme, is
  hers alone, and is deleted when her seat ends.
- *The worker's own agent.* Later, inside Etyme, with no mailbox.
