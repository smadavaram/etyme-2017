## Model mix, seven workstreams, and the launch order — 2026-11-22

The owner's instruction. It supersedes the earlier model rule.

**1. The model mix.** The build runs as a lead on the second tier with
workers on the third. The top tier is not the driver: it used 77% of
its allowance and caused the stalls. The lead keeps schema and money
work sequential under itself. The workers take the parallel surface
work. This relaxes the owner's earlier "no third-tier workers" rule, at
his direction. Model names are said to the owner, not written here.

**2. The work breakdown.** The remaining build is broken by the seven
chains of the agent activity spec (record 112): one workstream per
chain, each with its own acceptance test taken from the spec. An
activity counts only when it runs in production, on the ledger and
metered, inside its gates. Chains go in parallel except where schema or
money forces a sequence.

**3. The launch order.** Chains 4 (run the week) and 5 (money loop) for
Tapin first: that is the launch. Their records already pass audit; the
agents are what is missing: the chase, reconciliation, and the payroll
draft. Then chains 1 to 3 and 6. Chain 7 (the decision queue and the
agent control view) is the surface everything lands on. It ships with
the first launch, not last.

**The report.** Per chain: what is live in production, what is draft
only, what is not built, against the activity spec, not against code
written.

**The lead's reading.**
- A deploy still happens on a Saturday or a Sunday only (record 107).
  The launch is built in the week and goes out on the weekend.
- Schema and money sequence: chain 5's agents and chain 4's payroll
  draft share the month record and the payroll tables, so they are one
  sequence under the lead. Chain 7's surface, chain 4's chase and the
  checklists of chain 3 can run beside it.
- An agent that runs needs three things nothing has today: a runner on
  a schedule that writes to the ledger as the agent, a switch per
  tenant on the control view, and a meter. These are chain 7's first
  work, because every other chain lands on them.
- Still the owner's to say before chain 4's chase is built: whether a
  missing-timesheet chase is sent by the agent alone or by a person's
  yes (record 112, point 1).
