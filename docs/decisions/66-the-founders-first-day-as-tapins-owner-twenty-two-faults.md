## The founder's first day as Tapin's Owner: twenty-two faults, fixed — 2026-11-12 to 13

The founder signed in to Tapin as its Owner, invited three partners and
read every settings page. He sent a list of twenty-two faults in four
groups, and three things to keep. What a person sees now:

- **Sign-in (1 to 5).** The account buttons come first, then the email.
  A signed-in browser reads "Continue as <address>" and "Use another
  account", never a second sign-in form. A sign-in that did not finish
  says so with one Try again button. A person with a password can change
  it from the account menu.
- **Invitations (6 to 9).** An invitation that could not be emailed says
  so, with the reason and the next step, and never says "emailed".
  Superadmin reads the failed sends, recipient masked. "Copy invitation"
  gives a text with the sign-in address and no link that takes the seat.
  "Send the invitation again" re-sends for the same seat. The invite
  form is first on the page, can always be pressed, says "Email", and
  takes several addresses at once.
- **People and settings (10 to 18).** Every screen counts people the
  same way and says what it counts ("3 with access · 3 invited"). A
  person invited and never signed in is "Invited", not a member, and is
  withdrawn, not paused. Pause, Reinstate and End access are on the row
  with a reason. A role shows what it may do ("Everything", "M of N").
  The sentence about how people join is one sentence, read by every
  screen. Every row shows the sign-in address. A shared mailbox seat can
  be renamed by its company. Role and access level are explained once.
  A company with no teams reads a plain list, not an empty chart.
- **Labels and navigation (19 to 22).** "Automation" reads "Activity
  log". Settings' tabs are grouped People, Company and Records. "Needs a
  decision" and the other counters say what they count. "Import records"
  and "Read teams from your directory" each point to the other, and a
  source that cannot be turned on says what it waits on.
- **Kept, as he asked:** the "Can they see it?" check, Give access
  asking why and how far, and the log recording who, when and whether it
  can be undone.

Coordinator's calls on the way, each reversible by the founder in a
sentence:
- **A company renames only a person it alone knows.** A person with a
  seat elsewhere (live or ended), papers, weeks, expenses, a resume, a
  bench listing or a course on the record changes their own name under
  Your data. The name is one row every company reads.
- **Invitation acts have their own names in the log**: sent again,
  copied, withdrawn, declined, accepted, beside "invited".
- **A firm's Etyme address sends an app page to the address that works
  today**, read from the deployment's own settings.

**Built** by the architect, conversation and regulatory across 2026-11-12
and 13, with a walk of every fault on screens as six walkers. The release
check, the walk and the deploy are in record 67.
