Notes for whoever builds this

Six things specific to this screen.

Duplicates are one row, not five

The same person from three vendors collapses into a single row showing every vendor and every rate. That row is this screen's best feature — make it obvious.

Stalled roles sort to the top

Not newest first. A role open 27 days with three submissions is the thing that needs a decision today.

Quality score blocks publishing

Below a threshold the requirement cannot go out without an explicit override, and the override is recorded against whoever made it.

Every score opens to its evidence

No number stands alone. Clicking a match shows the quoted lines from the CV, and what was looked for and not found.

The check queue is work, not a notification

Ten human reviews a week with a running agreement rate. This is the only thing stopping the system grading its own homework.

Vendors rank by placement, never volume

Copperline sends 48 and places 2%. Trellis sends 14 and places 21%. Sorting by volume would put exactly the wrong vendor first.
