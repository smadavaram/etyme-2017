# The 100-Day Ladder

A printable 100-day and 1-year planning template for a 7th grader.

- `100-day-ladder.html` — open in any browser and print, or fill in by hand.

## How it works

- **Core vs. fun.** Core areas (3–4, same all year) get a target, a measure, and daily individual practice. Fun areas get 20 minutes and no target.
- **Individual feeds group.** Each core area names the group setting it feeds (team practice, Sunday writing class, Friday math class, classroom) and what the coach or teacher should notice by day 100.
- **Ramp, then hold.** 10 min/day in week 1, 20 in weeks 2–3, 30 from week 4 to day 100. Two scores: discipline (showed up) and consistency (streak).
- **Three cycles a year.** Days 1–100, 101–200, 201–300, then 65 days of rest and reset. Each cycle's day-100 target becomes the next cycle's day-1 baseline.

## Sheets

1. The fixed week (booked group sessions, pre-filled)
2. 100-Day Plan (one per cycle, four core areas + fun)
3. Daily tally (100-box grid with checkpoints at 25/50/75/100)
4. Checkpoints and day-100 review
5. 1-Year Plan (three cycles + reset, year-end table, optional 5-year line)
6. Sunday 10-minute review
