The 100-Day Ladder

Three 100-day cycles make a year. Each cycle raises the bar in the same core areas, so the skills stack instead of restarting. Individual practice is where the compounding happens. Group sessions are where it shows.

How the ladder works

Read once, together

Core vs. fun

Core areas get a target, a measure, and daily individual practice. Pick 3 or 4 and keep the same ones all year. Fun areas get 20 minutes and no target. No pressure, no score.

Individual feeds group

He already shows up disciplined in group settings. The solo work is the extra reps nobody else sees. Every core area names the group setting it feeds and what the coach or teacher should notice by day 100.

Ramp, then hold

Start small so the habit sticks: 10 minutes a day in week 1, 20 in weeks 2 and 3, then hold at 30 from week 4 to day 100. Two scores only: discipline (did I show up?) and consistency (the streak).

Sunday 10-minute review

Same questions every week
  1. How many days did I show up this week? (tally)
  2. Which core area moved the most? Which the least?
  3. What did the coach or teacher say or notice?
  4. What is the plan for the individual slots next week?
  5. What is my fun pick this week?
Notes

Parent's job is to ask the questions and write nothing. The kid owns the pen.
