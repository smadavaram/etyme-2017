Compliance & tenure

Tenure is counted per person at your company, across every supplier and every assignment, and a day on site counts once. At the limit the law is behind, Etyme blocks. Before it, it warns.

Key capabilities

Tenure per person

Across every supplier and assignment. A day on site counts once, and only days already served count.

Warns at three quarters

Of your cap. The default cap is eighteen months; the break in service, thirty days.

Blocks at the cap

No award, extension or start until the break is served, and the screen shows the date.

Insurance that stops submissions

Lapsed liability or workers’ compensation cover: nobody can be submitted through that supplier.

Visas chased

Notices at ninety, sixty and thirty days before expiry.

Rate parity, reported

The same role filled by two suppliers at two prices, and how far apart.

The complaint

“One person worked fourteen months through supplier A, three through B and two through C. That is nineteen months on our site, and none of the three suppliers can see the other two.”

The HR desk at a company with an eighteen-month limit

Each supplier’s count is honest and each is wrong, because each starts the clock the day it placed the person. The only party who could add the three together is the client, and the client is the one relying on the suppliers’ numbers.

What Etyme does

Tenure belongs to the person, not the assignment. Etyme adds up every day on site at your company across all suppliers, counts an overlapping day once, and counts only days already served. The cap and the break in service are your settings; the defaults are eighteen months and thirty days. At three quarters of the cap the row turns to a warning. At the cap, a new award, extension or start is blocked with a sentence that says why, and a returning person in a break period is told the date they are eligible.

The same desk watches the papers. General liability or workers’ compensation cover that lapses stops a supplier submitting anybody until it is back in date. A lapsed license stops that person starting. Visas are chased at ninety, sixty and thirty days. Every check writes a row with its outcome, even a pass, and every override keeps the name of whoever gave it.
